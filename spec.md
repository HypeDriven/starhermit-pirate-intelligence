# Pirate Intelligence

A pirate captain simulation with six named crew, persistent shared seas, direct orders, learned task preferences and constrained model decisions.

The default individual brain runs SmolLM2 135M entirely on the browser CPU. Share model weights and one inference lane across characters while keeping prompts, memories and preferences individual. Low system load is the default: target 10% of one core with short work slices, deliberate idle gaps and automatic suspension while hidden. Expose Low load, Balanced, Faster and Paused in the brain UI and remember the selection locally. These are inference pacing targets, not exact CPU caps.

Download and verify model weights once, cache them when browser storage allows, and show loading/thinking status. The default needs no localhost service, Python, API key or companion account. External OpenAI-compatible models remain an optional companion workflow. Built-in instincts keep the ship playable while a model loads, thinks, fails or is paused.

Each decision selects a legal crew task. Direct orders take priority, and the authority validates ownership, duties and emergency warrants. Discard interrupted work when switching worlds, hiding the game, changing providers or receiving a direct order. Model output never moves ships, spends gold or bypasses game rules. Pirate speech remains authored.

The expanded sea has nine destinations and four trading ports with shared stock, replenishment and scarcity prices. Spice, sailcloth and medicine share a cargo hold; same-port buy/sell cycles always lose money. Contracts include a daily repeatable delivery. Six ship fittings have three paid levels each. A captain’s log records exploration, renown, trades, expeditions and captures.

Crew coordinate limited duty stations and explain their assignments. Editable standing orders cover repair/rest thresholds, storm shelter and automatic hunting fire. Players can prefer duties, praise, discourage and forget habits. Trust, fatigue and injuries affect work; shared expeditions and boarding build bonds and bounded personal stories. Contextual preferences remain private to the captain and their authorised model officer.

Manual helm uses W/A/D or arrow keys, with held touch controls, wind, bounds and a two-second input timeout. H returns between manual helm and charted courses. Broadsides require a flank in manual mode. Round, chain and grape shot affect hull, rigging and boarding odds. Weakened NPC raiders can be boarded by two fit sailors; ownership, distance, party reservations and one-time prize rewards are authoritative. Sinking recalls all away crew and clears hazards.

Expeditions offer safe or risky encounters, with role, bonds, fatigue and injury affecting risk. Unanswered encounters choose the safe return, preventing a waiting party from stalling play. Rest and port medical care heal injuries. Fires, leaks and rigging damage need repair watches or port services. Welcome guidance, the sea atlas, a full journal, narrow-screen layouts, optional quiet ambience and procedural event sounds support the voyage loop. Repeated geometry uses instancing to reduce scene overhead. Local snapshots migrate in place; online sessions remain subject to the platform’s session/build and empty-world pause rules.

Anchored port ships are protected by harbour guns, so trading and menu use stay safe from raiders and player fire. Depart port before firing or boarding. Captains can name a sea, choose public listing, join an open sea, and retain their earlier world while moving between sessions.
