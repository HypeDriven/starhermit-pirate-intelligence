Six questionable souls. One small ship. Your orders.

You are the captain of a patched-up sloop on the Quarrelsome Sea. Set a standing directive (make your fortune, hunt raiders, play it safe) and your crew decide how to get there. Every sailor has a role, a temper and habits of their own, and they learn from the orders you give them.

- Chart a course between ports, islands and wrecks, trade spice, resupply and refit your hull.
- Send two of the crew ashore by longboat for timber, buried treasure or salvage, and manage the ship short-handed while they are gone.
- Take on contracts, bring rival ships within range and trade broadsides.
- Keep the chain of command: off-role orders need an officer's warrant and real danger.

Play local practice straight away, or sign in to create a persistent shared sea and invite other captains to join it with a room code.
